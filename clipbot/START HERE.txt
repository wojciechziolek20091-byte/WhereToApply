clipbot: turns long YouTube videos into TikTok clips
=====================================================

INSTALL (once)

  First unzip: right-click the clipbot zip file, choose "Extract All",
  then "Extract". Use the folder that opens, not the zip itself.

  Windows:  double-click "Install clipbot.cmd"
            If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click
            "More info", then "Run anyway".

  Mac:      right-click "Install clipbot.command" and choose Open,
            then click Open again. (A normal double-click is blocked
            the first time because clipbot is not from the App Store.)

  Wait until the window says "Done". It downloads about 1 GB the first
  time, so it can take a while on slow internet. clipbot then starts
  by itself and your browser opens.


START

  Double-click "clipbot" on your desktop. On Windows you can also press
  the Windows key and type clipbot. If you cannot find the icon, open
  the clipbot folder and double-click "Start clipbot".
  Your web browser opens the clipbot page by itself.
  The first time, it walks you through setup (about 10 minutes).

  Keep the black window open while clipbot works.
  Close it to stop clipbot.


USE

  Paste a YouTube link on the Overview page and press "Clip Video".
  Your clips appear under "Clips". Play them, approve them, done.

  clipbot starts in practice mode: it makes clips but does not post.
  Turn practice mode off in Settings when you like the clips.


SOMETHING WRONG?

  Take a photo of the black window and send it to whoever gave you
  clipbot. Your keys stay on this computer; never send the ".env" file.
